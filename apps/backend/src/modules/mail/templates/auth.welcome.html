Welcome to SourceWeft, the open-source NotebookLM alternative for deep knowledge work. We're building it so you can connect your sources, ask grounded questions, and create outputs that stay tied to verifiable citations.

A strong first session has three simple moves:

  • Connect your source space — upload PDFs, paste URLs, write notes, add YouTube links, or bring in tools like Notion, Google Drive, Gmail, and Slack.
  • Ask with evidence — select sources and ask questions. The answer should come back grounded, cited, and easy to verify.
  • Create from context — turn research into briefings, study guides, FAQs, timelines, drafts, Artifacts, and working files.

If you want the shortest path, open your dashboard, add one document you know well, and ask for a key claim, decision, date, risk, or quote. SourceWeft will show you how it read the source instead of asking you to trust a black box.

SourceWeft is self-hosted, multi-model, and built for teams that want control over their data, models, and knowledge workflows. Virtual FS and Skills give agents a real workspace when you are ready to go deeper.
